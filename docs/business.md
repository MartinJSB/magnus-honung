# Business notes

## Expected yield

- Swedish average is roughly **25–35 kg of honey per colony and year**; first-year colonies
  usually give much less (0–15 kg) because they are still building up.
- 4 colonies in a normal year: **~100–140 kg**.
- A standard Swedish honey jar holds **500 g (≈ 0.36 l)**. Honey weighs ~1.4 kg/l, so a true
  half-litre jar would hold ~700 g.

| Scenario       | kg total | 500 g jars |
| -------------- | -------: | ---------: |
| First season   |    20–60 |     40–120 |
| Normal year    |  100–140 |    200–280 |
| Very good year |  160–200 |    320–400 |

50 jars is a safe first-year number; 500 jars would need ~60 kg per colony and is not a planning
baseline.

## Pricing

Reference points (500 g):

- Imported supermarket honey: ~50–70 SEK.
- Average Swedish beekeeper price 2025: ~100 SEK (Biodlartips "Honungsbörsen").
- Premium local producers: 130–160 SEK.

**Current price:** 170 SEK retail for 500 g, the only size sold (no 250 g jar). Local,
hand-tapped honey with a story ("four hives and an old dream") justifies a premium price.

**School classes:** buy at 80 SEK, sell at 170 SEK → **90 SEK/jar (53 %) to the class**, minimum
20 jars.

The original idea (Magnus 50 SEK → class 100 SEK) gives the class 50 % but leaves Magnus ~40
SEK/jar after jar, lid and label (~6–10 SEK): half the market price, which undercuts other
beekeepers and makes the business hard to sustain.

| Normal year, 240 jars | Revenue to Magnus |
| --------------------- | ----------------: |
| All via classes @ 80  |        19 200 SEK |
| All retail @ 170      |        40 800 SEK |
| Half / half           |        30 000 SEK |

## Payment options

| Step    | Option                                | Cost                 | Notes                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 1 (now) | Order request + Swish on confirmation | Free                 | What v1 does. Fine for small volumes.           |
| 2       | Swish QR in confirmation email        | Free (private Swish) | Fewer manual steps, still no integration.       |
| 3       | Swish Företag / Swish Handel          | Bank fee per payment | Requires a registered business (enskild firma). |
| 4       | Stripe Checkout (cards, Klarna)       | ~1.5 % + ~2 SEK/txn  | Real web shop with shipping.                    |

Shipping glass jars by post is expensive and fragile, so local pickup, local delivery and class
sales are the natural channels to start with.

## Before selling (check locally)

- Register the apiary with the county board (Länsstyrelsen / Jordbruksverket).
- Ask the municipality's environmental office whether honey sales need food-business registration.
- Label: "Honung", net weight, best-before date, producer name/address, country of origin.
- Taxes: small-scale sales may count as hobby income; beyond that, an enskild firma is the simple
  route.
