# Design assets

The photographs in `apps/web/public/images` were supplied by the owner. The site uses `springa.jpg` without modifying the original; framing is handled by CSS.

`apps/web/public/images/honung.webp` is the owner's photo of Magnus's honey (original `honung.HEIC`), cropped to a 4:5 portrait around the jar and spoon and resized to 1200×1500 for the hero. Product jars are CSS packaging illustrations.
