# Design assets

The portrait photographs in `apps/web/public/images` were supplied by the owner. The redesign uses `springa.jpg` and `rhodos.jpg` without modifying the originals; framing is handled by CSS.

`apps/web/public/images/honey-still-life.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool. It is an illustrative mood photograph, not a photograph of Magnus's actual honey or apiary. Product jars are CSS packaging illustrations.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial still-life photograph for a premium Swedish small-scale honey website. Create a beautiful natural close-up of an open clear glass jar filled with golden honey on weathered pale oak, a wooden honey dipper resting diagonally over the mouth, a small piece of honeycomb beside it, and delicate meadow flowers at the edge. No label, no text, no branding. Quiet Scandinavian countryside mood. Warm late summer sunlight from the upper left, rich olive green blurred foliage background, tactile linen in foreground, exquisite realistic texture and restrained film grain. Portrait 4:5 composition, jar in lower-middle with foliage and light above, shallow depth of field. This is an illustrative mood photograph, not documentation of a real producer.
